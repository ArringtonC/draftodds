Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

DraftOdds (“the app,” “we,” “us”) is built privacy-first. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect. The short version: your draft data stays on your device, and we don’t have a server that receives it.

What we collect

Nothing that personally identifies you, off your device.

  • Draft & app data (your mock/live drafts, saved teams, grades, settings) is stored locally on your device only. We do not upload it, and we cannot see it.
  • No account. DraftOdds has no sign-up, no login, and no user profiles.
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs are embedded in the app, and the app does not track you across other apps or websites (see our App Privacy “Data Not Collected” declaration and the bundled privacy manifest).

Purchases

DraftOdds offers an optional auto-renewing subscription (the Season Pass). Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your name, payment card, or billing address — Apple confirms only whether a valid purchase exists so the app can unlock Pro features. Apple’s handling of your purchase is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Data you choose to share

If you tap Share on a grade card or challenge link, iOS’s standard share sheet opens and you choose where it goes (Messages, social, etc.). That action, and anything you send, is initiated and controlled by you — DraftOdds does not send it on your behalf or keep a copy on a server.

Sports data in the app

DraftOdds ships with NFL player projections, rankings, and average-draft-position data compiled from public and licensed sources. This data flows into the app; no personal information about you flows back out to those sources.

Children’s privacy

DraftOdds is intended for a general audience of fantasy-football players and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds features that change what data is handled (for example, optional analytics), we will update this policy and the date above before that version ships.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@draftodds.app.
