Frequently asked

What does DraftOdds actually do?

Rankings tell you who's good. DraftOdds tells you who to draft next. On every pick it reads the roster you've actually built and names the one player who raises your championship odds the most right now, with the reason and the odds he survives to your next pick.

Is it free?

You can browse the rankings and watch the full demo draft for free, with no account. Running the draft coach on your own draft is part of DraftOdds Pro, which starts with a 3-day free trial.

How much is Pro, and how do I cancel?

$24.99 per year after the 3-day free trial. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Cancel anytime in Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions on your device. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I paid but the app still shows the paywall.

Tap Restore purchase on the paywall while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. If it still doesn't unlock, email us and mention the Apple Account region you purchased in.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all purchases and refunds for App Store subscriptions. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue refunds directly, but tell us what went wrong so we can fix it.

Does it connect to Yahoo, ESPN, or Sleeper?

Not yet. You run a mock against bots, or mark picks as they happen in your real draft and DraftOdds keeps up. League import is on the list.

Do you support PPR, half-PPR, and standard?

Yes. Set your scoring when you start a draft, along with league size, your pick slot, roster shape, and Superflex. Scoring changes the math, not just a label.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. There's no account and no sign-up — your drafts live on your phone. See the Privacy Policy.

Are the projections guaranteed?

No, and we won't pretend otherwise. It's a transparent model built on real data, not a fake win-probability badge. Player performance is uncertain; DraftOdds is for entertainment and information.

Is this gambling?

No. DraftOdds accepts no wagers and is not a gambling service. Any betting-market information shown is context only.
