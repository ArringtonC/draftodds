The difference

Not the best player.
The best pick.

Those are two different players almost every round, and the gap between them is where drafts are won and lost.

A rankings list says

“Here are the top 200 players, in order.” The same list for you, for the person picking first, and for the person who already has four running backs. It cannot see your team, and it does not change when the board does.

DraftOdds says

“Take Breece Hall now. Five receivers just went, your tier is thin, and he has an 11% chance of lasting until your next pick.” Then it recalculates the moment somebody else picks.
